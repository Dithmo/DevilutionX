PBR detail, roughness and normal maps for the 3D actor baker.

Derived from ambientCG materials (https://ambientcg.com), licensed CC0 1.0 (public domain):
  Metal038, Metal054A  -> metal_*.jpg   (plus a procedural hammered-dent normal)
  Leather037           -> leather_*.jpg
  Fabric031            -> fabric_*.jpg

Rebuilt with scripts/prep_textures.py (in the arpg-forge skill) from the 1K-JPG downloads.
Albedo is stored as grayscale detail (mean ~0.8) and tinted by each look's colours at runtime.
