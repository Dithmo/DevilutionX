What changed

Real lighting. One warm key light from the upper left (where the game's light comes from), a cool rim from behind, and image-based reflections on the metal. Plate gets real specular highlights and soft self-shadows instead of painted gradients.

Real anatomy and motion. Legs use two-bone IK with heel strike and toe-off. The pelvis bobs, rolls and twists against the shoulders. The cloak is a mesh driven by a travelling wave. The attack is keyframed: wind-up, strike, follow-through.

Same pipeline cost. Everything is generated from the class's look data at load, so there are no model files or art assets. Bake time is shown above. More frames per animation cost only memory.
