What changed in the PBR pass

Texture maps. Scanned CC0 sets (worn and scratched metal, leather, wool) were reduced to grey detail, roughness and normal maps, then tinted by the class colours, so any theme can reuse them. Hand-beaten dents were added to the metal normals. Box projection maps them onto parts that have no texture coordinates.

Wear and grime. Plate edges are rolled, rubbed and more polished. About twenty spheres follow the skeleton and shade every pixel. Where parts crowd together (under pauldrons, inside the arms, between the legs, at the feet), the surface gets darker, dirtier and less metallic, in any pose.

Moody reflections. The metal reflects a dim stone room with torch hot-spots that match the key light, instead of a white studio. The cloak is wool with a soft fabric sheen and a dirty, frayed hem.
